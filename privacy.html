Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

UniHive tracks Nigerian university admissions and sends you personalized alerts based on the information you provide during sign-up. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address
  • Your qualification, target programme, preferred region, target schools and course interests

We collect this to personalize the alerts we send you — nothing more.

How we use it

  • To send you an email verifying your address after sign-up
  • To match your profile against admission updates and send you relevant alerts
  • To send a short weekly check-in if nothing new comes up for your profile

What we don't do

  • We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • We never link you to third-party news sites or aggregators — only official university and JAMB sources
  • We never send unsolicited emails outside what you signed up for

Your control

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. One click removes you permanently — no confirmation step, no dark patterns.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at hello@unihive.com.ng.
