How it works

Set it up once. We take it from there.

No manual checking. No WhatsApp groups. No missed forms. Tell us what you need — we monitor, you act.

1

Tell us about yourself

Answer 5 quick questions — your qualification, target programme (UTME, Direct Entry, PG or Professional), preferred region, and course interests. Takes under a minute.

2

We check every day

Our agent scans university portals and official sources every morning, filtering only what matches your profile — your programme, your region, your courses.

3

You hear from us when it matters

The moment a portal opens or something changes for your profile, you get an alert that same day. Nothing to report that week? We still check in every Monday so you know we're on it — no daily noise either way.
