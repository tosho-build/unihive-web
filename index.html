How it works

From signup to your first alert in 60 seconds

No manual checking. No WhatsApp groups. No missed forms. Tell us what you need — we monitor, you act.

1

Tell us about yourself

Answer 5 quick questions — your qualification, target programme (UTME, Direct Entry, PG or Professional), preferred region, and course interests. Takes under a minute.

2
